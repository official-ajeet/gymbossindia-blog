If you're running your gym off a register at the front desk, or an Excel sheet someone updates "when they get time," you already know where this breaks down. Here's what it actually costs you — and what changes when you move off it.

## Where a register or Excel actually fails

- **No one can search it fast.** Finding whether a specific member's payment is up to date means flipping pages or scrolling a spreadsheet, while they're standing at the counter.
- **Renewals get missed, not skipped on purpose.** Nobody forgets to renew a member deliberately — a expiring membership just doesn't announce itself in a register. It gets noticed when the member's card doesn't work anymore, which is the worst possible moment to notice.
- **It breaks with more than one person updating it.** Two staff members editing the same Excel file, or one register with illegible handwriting from a different shift, means the "source of truth" often isn't.
- **There's no backup.** A spilled coffee or a corrupted file, and months of member history is gone.

## What switching actually looks like

Moving off a register isn't a multi-week IT project — it's usually:

1. **Import your existing list.** Your current member names, phone numbers, and join dates go in as a CSV — you shouldn't have to retype hundreds of members by hand.
2. **Members get a QR code.** Front desk scans it (or members self-scan) at check-in — no more searching a register for their name.
3. **Staff gets a dashboard, not a spreadsheet.** Who's expiring this week, who paid in cash today, who hasn't shown up in 10 days — all visible without anyone manually compiling it.

## What you gain that a register can't do

- **Automatic expiry alerts**, days before a membership lapses — not after.
- **A live revenue view** — this month's collections at a glance, not reconstructed from a stack of receipts at month-end.
- **Every staff member sees the same data**, in real time, from any device.

## "My staff isn't very tech-savvy"

This is the most common reason gym owners delay switching — and usually the smallest actual barrier. A QR check-in is simpler for staff to use than a register, not harder: scan, done. Most gyms are fully set up and checking in members within a couple of minutes of signing up, not days.
